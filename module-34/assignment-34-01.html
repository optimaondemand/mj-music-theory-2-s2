What to do
1
Compose an original 12-bar blues melody. This is not a recreation of an existing song. Use the correct I7-IV7-V7 arrangement.
2
Shape it AAB: an A phrase (call, bars 1-4), an A′ phrase (variation, bars 5-8), and a B phrase (response, bars 9-12).
3
Use blue notes expressively. Include at least two of the three: ♭3, ♭5, ♭7.
4
Record audio (required). Play or sing it live, or use File > Export > Audio (.mp3 or .wav). First open View > Mixer and adjust at least two: volume balance, instrument sound, or tempo.
5
Composer statement (4-6 sentences in the text box): your call-and-response structure; where you placed blue notes and why; the expressive quality you aimed for; which Mixer settings you changed and why; and, using these five requirements as your criteria, one thing you would still revise.
Turn in
Upload your melody as a MuseScore file or a PDF of staff notation, and your audio. Put your statement in the text box. All three are required.
Full credit: a genuine attempt at all five requirements, including audio and the self-critique. Your teacher leaves a brief comment with one strength and one suggestion.
Before you submit
☐My melody has AAB shape and at least two blue notes.
☐My audio is attached and my Mixer changes are named.
☐My statement ends with one thing I would revise.
