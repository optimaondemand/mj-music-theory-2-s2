How does a sudden key change feel different from a smooth one?
Two ways to change key
DirectA sudden lift, with no chord to carry you across.
PivotA smooth turn, through a chord both keys share.
What to post
1
Think of a pop song where the key suddenly goes up near the end. That sudden shift is a direct modulation. If you cannot think of one, search YouTube for “songs with key changes.”
2
Post the song and artist. Say where the key change happens and what it feels like emotionally at that moment.
3
Imagine a pivot. How would the emotional effect change if the same key change used a smooth pivot chord instead? Would the song be more or less effective?
4
Reply to one classmate. Agree with their emotional assessment and add a detail, or respectfully disagree and explain why you hear the key change differently.
Grading
Post your answers, then reply to one classmate.
Full credit: a specific song and moment, a direct versus pivot comparison, and one specific reply. A thin post earns partial credit. No post earns 0.
