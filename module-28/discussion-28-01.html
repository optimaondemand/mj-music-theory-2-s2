What to do
1
Listen to the opening of Bach's Little Fugue in G minor. Search YouTube; many public-domain recordings exist.
2
Describe the subject in your own words: its rhythm, its shape (rising or falling), and where it starts (low or high). How many times do you hear it enter in the first 30 seconds?
3
Answer the question above. Name one specific thing that stays the same across all the entries you heard.
4
Reply to one classmate. Do you agree with what they said stays the same? Add one more observation about the subject's identity that they did not mention.
Turn in
Full credit: a substantive post with specific details, the number of entries, and one unifying feature, plus one specific reply. A thin post earns partial credit. No post earns 0.
Your teacher will post two or three examples from the recording to anchor the discussion.
